# Summit Mutual (fictional carrier) — Small Commercial Underwriting Guidelines

Version 2026.2. Applies to the Small Commercial Package (Property + General Liability).
This carrier is fictional and created for a demo project.

## 1. Target Market

1.1 We write small and mid-size businesses with annual revenue between $250,000 and $25,000,000.

1.2 Preferred classes: offices (professional services, IT, insurance agencies), retail stores,
restaurants without deep fryers or with an automatic extinguishing system, light assembly,
warehouses storing non-hazardous goods, and medical offices without surgery.

1.3 New ventures (under 3 years in business) are acceptable only with underwriter referral and an
experienced owner. Businesses under 1 year in business are declined.

## 2. Prohibited Classes (Decline)

2.1 The following classes are outside appetite and must be declined:
fireworks or explosives manufacturing or storage, cannabis growing or dispensaries,
crypto-currency mining, demolition contractors, nightclubs and bars where alcohol exceeds 50% of
sales, amusement parks, and waste or recycling facilities.

2.2 Any business that manufactures or stores hazardous chemicals in bulk is declined.

## 3. Property Requirements

3.1 Maximum total insured value (building plus contents) per location is $15,000,000.
Values above $10,000,000 require referral to a senior underwriter.

3.2 Frame construction buildings built before 1960 require referral and a recent inspection.

3.3 Buildings over 25,000 square feet or with total insured value above $5,000,000 must be
sprinklered. Unsprinklered risks above this threshold are referred.

## 4. Catastrophe Exposure

4.1 Locations in high hurricane zones (coastal Florida, Louisiana, coastal Texas) require referral
and a wind deductible of at least 2%.

4.2 Locations in high wildfire zones (parts of California, Colorado) require referral.

4.3 Locations in FEMA high-risk flood zones are written without flood coverage; flood must be
placed separately.

## 5. Loss History

5.1 Three or more claims in the last 5 years require referral.

5.2 Total paid losses above $250,000 in the last 5 years require referral.

5.3 Any single loss above $500,000, or any fire loss in the last 3 years, requires referral with
a written explanation of what was fixed.

## 6. Submission Completeness

6.1 A submission is complete only when it contains: the insured's legal name, a location address,
business description, annual revenue, building and contents values, construction type, year
built, the lines requested, and a requested effective date.

6.2 Loss runs for 5 years are required. If loss runs are missing, request them from the broker
before quoting.

6.3 Effective dates must be at least 7 days in the future to allow underwriting review.

## 7. Turnaround and Prioritisation

7.1 Target turnaround is 2 business days for complete, in-appetite submissions.

7.2 Prioritise submissions from preferred classes with clean loss history and effective dates
within 30 days; these are most likely to bind.
